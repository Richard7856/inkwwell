# 🇺🇸 English (United States) — idioma predeterminado

**App name** (30)
```
InkAR
```

**Short description** (80)
```
Your tattoo comes alive in AR. Point a camera and watch your memory play.
```

**Full description** (4000)
```
InkAR turns your tattoo into a doorway to a memory.

This is not a tattoo try-on app or a filter. Your real tattoo — the one already
on your skin — gets linked to an augmented reality experience. Anyone who points
a camera at it sees that experience appear, anchored to your skin and following
your movement.

BRING YOUR MEMORY TO LIFE

Upload a photo of what the tattoo is about — your dog, your grandmother, that
afternoon — and tell us its story in a few words. AI turns it into a short
video, and that video is what appears over your tattoo from then on.

The tattoo you carry stops being only a drawing and becomes the door to the
thing it was drawn for.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Take a photo of your tattoo
2. We measure whether it will track well, and tell you before you spend anything
3. Upload the photo of your memory and write its story
4. In a few minutes your video is ready and your tattoo is activated

From then on, anyone who points their camera at your tattoo sees your memory.
They don't need to install anything: opening your link in a browser is enough.

NO TATTOO? YOU CAN STILL TRY IT

InkAR recognizes any image with enough visual detail — a book cover, a poster,
a playing card, a drawing. Activate one and scan it: you go through the exact
same flow a tattooed user does, not a canned demo.

CREDITS

Generating a video costs one credit. You can buy credits inside the app; your
first one is half price. There is also a free catalog of animated 3D models so
you can see how it looks without paying.

WHAT YOU NEED

A healed tattoo, at least 4 cm across, with clear visual detail, and good
lighting when you register it. Very small or very simple line work can be harder
to recognize — the app measures yours and tells you before you spend a credit.

PRIVACY

Camera images never leave your phone while scanning: recognition happens
entirely on your device. The photo of your memory is sent to our AI provider
only when you ask for a video. You can delete your account and all your data
whenever you want, from inside the app or at https://inkar.app/eliminar-cuenta
```

**Instrucciones adicionales** (van en inglés):
```
Scanning requires no account. To try the AR without a tattoo, open the app and
tap "Pruébalo sin tatuaje" (Try it without a tattoo) on the home screen: it
shows a marker image to display on another screen or print, and then opens the

To test tattoo activation, sign in first:
1. On the home screen tap "Activar mi tatuaje" (Activate my tattoo)
2. On the sign-in screen tap "Prefiero usar contraseña" (I prefer a password)
   — the default method sends a 6-digit code by email; the password option is
   behind that link
3. Enter the email and password provided above

FREE CREDIT FOR REVIEWERS
Generating a video costs one credit. To get one at no charge, go to "Mis
créditos" (My credits) and redeem the code:

    SHIPATON

Then activate a tattoo (or any detailed image) and choose "Anima tu recuerdo"
(Bring your memory to life). Video generation takes 1-3 minutes.

Account deletion can be tested at https://inkar.app/eliminar-cuenta with the
same credentials, without installing the app.

There is no two-factor authentication, biometrics, or subscription required.
```

⚠️ El paso 2 es indispensable: el acceso con contraseña está detrás de un
enlace. Sin la instrucción, el revisor ve la pantalla del código por correo, se
atora y rechaza.

⚠️ El código `SHIPATON` es lo que convierte "no pude probar la función principal"
en "la probé completa". Es canjeable una sola vez por cuenta.

# Capturas — qué tomar y en qué orden

Play muestra las primeras dos en el resultado de búsqueda: **esas dos deciden si
alguien instala.** Mínimo 2, máximo 8, entre 320 y 3840 px por lado.

| # | Pantalla | Dónde se toma | Por qué esta |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **El video sobre el tatuaje real, en cámara** | Teléfono, app instalada | Es el producto. No se puede simular ni sustituir |
| 2 | **"Anima tu recuerdo"** con foto e historia escritas | Teléfono o navegador | Explica de dónde sale el video sin leer una palabra |
| 3 | Veredicto de calidad con sus medidas | Teléfono o navegador | Es lo que nadie más hace: medimos antes de cobrar |
| 4 | Inicio con el logotipo | Cualquiera | Marca |
| 5 | Mis tatuajes con dos o tres activados | Teléfono | Muestra que es tuyo y permanente |
| 6 | Créditos con los paquetes | Teléfono | Play mira que el cobro se declare y se vea |

Las de interfaz (2 a 6) se pueden tomar del navegador en el teléfono, entrando a
inkar.app — no hace falta esperar a que la v4 esté publicada. **La 1 no**: exige
la app y un tatuaje activado.
